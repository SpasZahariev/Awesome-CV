\lettersection{About Me}
I am a Software Engineering Lead with 7+ years building backend and distributed systems across fintech, and I am writing about the Senior Robotics Middleware Engineer role on the 4NE-1 Core Robot Software team. My background is not in robotics middleware specifically, but in the discipline the role also demands: getting many independent, high-throughput components to communicate correctly and fast, in production, with zero tolerance for silent failure. At JPMorgan Chase I redesigned a Java messaging service on Kafka with async processing that cut turnaround from 30 minutes to under 5 for 150K+ messages/hour, and at EPAM I lead a 7-engineer team keeping 500K+ metadata updates per day consistent across four distinct systems, including a REST-to-SOAP gateway bridging two incompatible protocol worlds.

\lettersection{Why NEURA Robotics?}
Building a humanoid software platform where perception, planning, and control must share one coherent, low-latency communication fabric is a systems problem I find genuinely exciting, distinct from the application-layer work I have shipped most of my career but built on the same fundamentals: message contracts, timing guarantees, and reliability under load. I am drawn to the ambition of building this middleware layer for a production humanoid robot rather than a lab prototype, and to the chance to work at the boundary between hard real-time control and distributed, cloud-connected services, a boundary I have worked from the distributed-systems side and want to push closer to the metal.

\lettersection{Why Me?}
C++ is in my daily toolkit alongside Java and Python, and my production experience is in exactly the problems this role reframes at the middleware level: designing service abstractions across heterogeneous systems (the REST/SOAP gateway at EPAM), architecting event-driven pipelines for throughput and low latency (Kafka at JPMorgan), and running distributed infrastructure reliably on Docker, Kubernetes, and cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP). I have not yet worked directly with ROS 2, DDS, or OROCOS RTT, and I say that plainly rather than overstate it, but I learn unfamiliar systems fast under pressure, as shown by redesigning EPAM's sync pipeline onto Azure with zero integrity incidents. I would welcome the opportunity to bring that same rigor to NEURA's humanoid stack and to grow into the specific middleware ecosystems this role owns.

\end{cvletter}
