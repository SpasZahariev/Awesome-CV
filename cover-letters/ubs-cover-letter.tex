\lettersection{About Me}
I am a Software Engineering Lead with 7+ years across fintech and large-scale consulting, and I am applying for the Senior Cloud Architect role on the new Cloud Center of Excellence team. At EPAM in Zurich I architected and led the migration of a full sync pipeline off on-prem infrastructure onto Azure, redesigning secure batch ingestion around Blob Storage and Azure Functions with zero integrity incidents, while owning architecture end to end from design through production for a system that keeps 500K+ metadata updates per day consistent across Apache Atlas, Azure, and mainframe DB2. That is the same discipline this role asks for: cloud platform design that has to hold up under regulatory scrutiny, not just work in a demo.

\lettersection{Why UBS Card Center?}
Standing up a Cloud Center of Excellence inside a card center means the governance framework is the product: landing zones, identity, and network design that every downstream SaaS and PaaS workload inherits, under PCI/DSS and data-residency constraints that leave no room for shortcuts. I have spent my career in exactly that kind of environment, where cross-system data integrity and auditability were non-negotiable, and I would welcome bringing that mindset to Glattbrugg to help shape the hybrid Azure/AWS blueprint from the ground up.

\lettersection{Why Me?}
My stack maps directly onto the role. I have hands-on production experience with Microsoft Azure and AWS, plus Google Cloud, and I work daily with Terraform, Docker, and Kubernetes to design and automate the platform services underneath. I have led a 7-engineer team building the REST-to-SOAP gateway and sync architecture that gave external auditors safe access to mainframe data, and I have coordinated roadmaps directly with product owners and internal customers as the engineer who owns both the architecture and its delivery. I would welcome the chance to discuss how I can help build UBS Card Center's cloud governance framework.
