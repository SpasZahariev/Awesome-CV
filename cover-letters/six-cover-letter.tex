\lettersection{About Me}
I am a Software Engineering Lead with 7+ years building backend systems across regulated finance and large-scale consulting, specialising in Java/Spring Boot, cloud delivery on Azure, and event-driven architecture. I am applying for the Messaging Platform Engineer, Cloud \& Containers role at SIX. My career has centred on high-throughput messaging and data-integrity platforms where reliability is non-negotiable: at JPMorgan Chase I redesigned a Java service with Kafka and async processing that cut messaging latency by approximately 85\%, from 30 minutes to under 5 minutes for 150K+ messages per hour. At EPAM in Zurich I currently lead a 7-engineer team owning a Java/Spring Boot sync service keeping 500K+ metadata updates per day consistent across Apache Atlas, Azure, and mainframe DB2 systems.

\lettersection{Why SIX?}
SIX drives the transformation of financial markets, and the shift from an on-premises IBM MQ Appliance to a hybrid messaging portfolio spanning OpenShift and Azure is exactly the kind of infrastructure evolution I want to contribute to. The challenge of expanding business-critical messaging across containers while evaluating complementary cloud-native technologies -- all within a regulated environment where downtime has real market consequences -- matches my background in shipping resilient, observable systems under strict compliance constraints. Being already based in Zurich, I can engage fully with the team from day one.

\lettersection{Why Me?}
My experience maps directly onto the role's requirements. I have production Java/Spring Boot experience building and leading messaging-adjacent services at scale, hands-on Azure delivery including migrating pipelines to Azure Functions with zero integrity incidents, and container orchestration through Docker and Kubernetes. My skills include Terraform for infrastructure as code, Jenkins CI/CD, and event-driven architecture with Kafka -- the same patterns that underpin modern cloud-native messaging platforms. While my direct experience is with Kafka rather than IBM MQ, the posting notes that experience need not be limited to IBM MQ, and I have a strong track record of rapidly mastering new enterprise technologies within regulated environments. I would welcome the chance to discuss how I can help SIX build its next-generation hybrid messaging portfolio.
