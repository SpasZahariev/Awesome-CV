\lettersection{About Me}
I am a Software Engineering Lead with 7+ years delivering production software at every layer of the modern tech stack, and I am applying for the Software Engineer III, AI/ML, DSP/HTP Optimisation role on the Platforms and Devices team. My path has taken me from core systems work at JPMorgan Chase, where I cut end-to-end message-processing latency by ~85\% (30min to under 5min) at 150K+ messages per hour, to leading a 7-engineer team at EPAM in Zurich that owns a data-lineage pipeline that reconciles 500K+ entities per day across 12 source systems. Every day I build and deploy corporate applications that interact with LLMs in production.

\lettersection{Why Google?}
Optimizing the end-to-end performance of ML models under strict power and thermal budgets is exactly the kind of problem I want to spend my career on: it sits at the intersection of machine learning and system architecture, and I have been drawn to that layer of the stack for a long time. I customize Linux distros, overclock hardware, and host and fine-tune LLM models locally with llama.cpp to squeeze every bit of performance out of my machines. I know DSP and HTP at a high level today, and I am the engineer who will get his hands dirty to master them: I have repeatedly shown that I learn fast when given the opportunity. The Platforms and Devices team builds the software that makes computing faster and more seamless for billions of users, and I would be proud to help define efficiency standards on next-generation hardware there.

\lettersection{Why Me?}
The listing's requirements map directly onto my experience. I have solid industry delivery in Python, and I built a RAG agent at EPAM that saves investment-committee members an average of 2 hours of preparation per pitch meeting. My Google Certified Machine Learning Engineer certification gave me hands-on skills in model deployment, evaluation, and MLOps, which I have practiced in GCP sandbox environments with Vertex AI and BigQuery, while I run Cloud Functions in production. With a full-stack delivery track record (Java, Python, TypeScript, Kubernetes, Kafka) and a history of wringing performance out of constrained systems, I bring the rigor this role needs for power profiling and hardware-specific optimization on HTP and DSP.
