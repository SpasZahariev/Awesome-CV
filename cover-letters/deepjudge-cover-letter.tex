My stack maps directly to what you need. I work daily in Python and TypeScript, with deep hands-on experience in LLMs, RAG pipelines, agent design, and machine learning (TensorFlow/Keras). I have shipped production systems at scale: a Kafka-driven async service that cut messaging latency by 85\% for 150K+ messages per hour at JPMorgan Chase, and an automated data-lineage pipeline spanning 4 analytics warehouses at EPAM. I am comfortable evaluating emerging LLM techniques and bringing them into production, and I have a track record of working directly with non-technical stakeholders -- from building live dashboards for 10+ analysts to serving on expert-assessment committees and mentoring engineers. I would welcome the chance to discuss how I can help DeepJudge turn its platform into high-value AI solutions that law firms depend on.
