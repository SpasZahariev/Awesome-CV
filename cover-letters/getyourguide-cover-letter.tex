\lettersection{About Me}
I am a Software Engineering Lead with 7+ years building backend systems that stay correct at scale, and I am applying for the Senior Software Engineer role on the Localization Platform team. At EPAM in Zurich I lead a 7-engineer team owning a Java/Spring Boot sync service that keeps 500K+ metadata updates per day consistent across Apache Atlas, Azure, and mainframe DB2/Postgres, plus a REST-to-SOAP gateway bridging modern and legacy systems. Before that, at JPMorgan Chase I redesigned a Kafka-based messaging service handling 150K+ messages/hour, cutting turnaround from 30 minutes to under 5. Platform work that many teams depend on and cannot see is exactly what I gravitate toward.

\lettersection{Why GetYourGuide?}
A localization platform is a multiplier: get it right and every product team ships to every market without re-solving translation, formatting, and content-sync problems themselves. That is the same shape of problem I have spent my career on, building shared infrastructure (data-lineage pipelines, cross-system sync services, secure gateways) that other teams build on top of without thinking about it. GetYourGuide's customer-first, experiment-led culture and its investment in engineers, mentorship, training budget, AI tooling, tells me this is a place that treats platform engineering as a craft rather than a cost center, and Zurich is where I am already based and want to keep building.

\lettersection{Why Me?}
I work daily in Java and Python, backed by microservices, event-driven architecture (Kafka), containerization (Docker/Kubernetes), and both Azure and GCP. I have redesigned batch and streaming pipelines for zero-downtime migrations, built REST/SOAP integration layers across heterogeneous systems, and led architecture-to-production ownership of services with strict consistency requirements, skills that map directly onto keeping a localization platform reliable as GetYourGuide's catalog and language coverage grow. I would welcome the chance to discuss how I can help scale the Localization Platform team's infrastructure.

\end{cvletter}
