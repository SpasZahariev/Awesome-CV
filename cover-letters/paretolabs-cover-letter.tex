\lettersection{About Me}
I am a Software Engineering Lead with 7+ years across fintech and large-scale consulting, and I am applying for the Senior Backend Developer with DevOps Expertise role at ParetoLabs. My work spans backend systems (Java, Python), full-stack delivery, and owning infrastructure end-to-end, from architecture through production operation. At EPAM in Zurich I lead a 7-engineer team building a Java/Spring Boot sync service that keeps 500K+ metadata updates per day consistent across Apache Atlas, Azure, and mainframe DB2 systems, work where CI/CD discipline, monitoring, and environment reliability are not an afterthought but the foundation everything else runs on.

\lettersection{Why ParetoLabs?}
Building state-of-the-art AI applications, LLM-driven agents and co-pilots, and turning them into dependable production infrastructure rather than one-off demos is exactly the problem I want to work on. I have spent the last few years deep in LLM, RAG, and agent work while also carrying full ownership of the pipelines and environments that ship them, so I understand both sides of what ParetoLabs needs: the AI application itself, and the CI/CD, deployment, and monitoring backbone that keeps it running reliably for clients. Working inside a small, multicultural studio where one engineer owns that whole path, from prototype to production, is a scope I want.

\lettersection{Why Me?}
The stack maps directly onto the role. I work daily in Python and Java, with hands-on React and Angular experience from building WebSocket-driven dashboards at JPMorgan Chase. On the DevOps side, I have redesigned secure batch ingestion on Azure (Blob Storage, Azure Functions) to retire on-prem infrastructure risk with zero integrity incidents, and I work regularly with Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, and both AWS and Google Cloud (Vertex AI, BigQuery, Cloud Functions). I have also served on hiring and promotion panels and mentored engineers throughout my time at EPAM, so collaborating across disciplines and helping a small team level up is second nature. I would welcome the chance to discuss how I can help build and run ParetoLabs' AI applications end to end.
