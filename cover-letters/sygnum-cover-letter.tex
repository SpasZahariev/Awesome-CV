\lettersection{About Me}
I am a Software Engineering Lead with 7+ years building backend and full-stack systems across regulated finance, and I am applying for the Senior Software Engineer, AI role on the Client Lifecycle \& Banking Value Stream team. My career has been split between two worlds that this position brings together: rigorous banking engineering at JPMorgan Chase, and production AI/ML delivery. \lettersection{Why Sygnum?}
Sygnum's ambition to make AI infrastructure inside a regulated bank, not a collection of proofs-of-concept, is exactly the problem I want to work on. I have spent years shipping systems where regulatory exposure and data integrity are first-class constraints, and I believe the hard part of the Sygnum AI Hub is not the prompt, it is making agents dependable enough to sit next to core banking processes. Building a trusted gateway between traditional and digital asset banking, from Zurich where I am already based, is a mission I would be glad to commit to for the long term.

\lettersection{Why Me?}
The stack maps directly onto my experience. I work daily in Java, Python, and TypeScript, and my recent work is deep in LLMs, RAG pipelines, and agent design, backed by microservices, event-driven architecture (Kafka), containerization (Docker/Kubernetes), and both Azure and GCP (Vertex AI, BigQuery). I have hands-on React experience from building WebSocket-driven dashboards at JPMorgan, and I am comfortable connecting AI agents to real banking systems and data sources, precisely the integration and Model Context Protocol work this role centers on. I would welcome the chance to discuss how I can help turn the Sygnum AI Hub into production infrastructure the bank can rely on.
